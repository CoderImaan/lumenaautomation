Where to begin

Choose work that benefits from a better flow.

Automation is most useful when the process is understood, the desired outcome is clear and someone remains accountable when the normal path cannot continue.

01

Process discovery

Map triggers, inputs, decisions, handoffs and exceptions before selecting tools or automating steps.

02

Integration and orchestration

Connect APIs and business systems around an agreed process, with clear ownership of data and failure handling.

03

AI-assisted work

Consider AI for tasks such as classification, extraction or drafting when appropriate, with human review for decisions that need it.

Designed for operations

Build visibility into the workflow.

  • Define what success and failure look like for each step.
  • Keep access, data handling and audit needs in view during design.
  • Provide a path for exceptions and human intervention.
  • Document how the workflow is monitored and maintained.

AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Whether AI is appropriate, and what review controls are needed, depends on the process, data and risk involved.
