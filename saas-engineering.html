Engineering principles

Keep the product maintainable as it grows.

  • Choose architecture and technology to fit the product’s constraints.
  • Make integrations and data ownership explicit.
  • Consider accessibility, security and performance throughout delivery.
  • Prefer incremental releases that can be reviewed against real requirements.

Delivery scope, hosting choices, maintenance and support arrangements are defined for each engagement. We do not publish standard timelines or guarantees without understanding the work.
