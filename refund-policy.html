Refund Policy

Clear and transparent billing and refund guidelines

Last Updated: 05 May 2026

This Refund Policy applies to services offered by Lumena Automation and Budget Manager.

1. Free Trial

If offered, users are encouraged to evaluate the product during the trial period before purchasing.

2. No Refund Policy

  • All payments are non-refundable unless otherwise stated.
  • We do not provide refunds for partial usage or unused subscriptions.

3. Exceptions

Refunds may be considered only if:

  • Duplicate payment occurred
  • Technical issue prevents service usage and is unresolved

4. Cancellation

  • Users can cancel subscriptions anytime.
  • Cancellation will stop future billing.
  • Access continues until the end of the billing period.

5. Payment Disputes

For billing issues, please contact us before raising disputes with payment providers.
